Stephen Curry Timeline

  • 1988 - Born in Akron, Ohio, to Dell Curry (former NBA player) and Sonya Curry.
  • 2006 - Enrolls at Davidson College after being overlooked by major Division I programs due to his size.
  • 2008 - Leads Davidson to a historic NCAA Tournament Elite Eight run, capturing national attention.
  • 2009 - Drafted 7th overall in the NBA Draft by the Golden State Warriors.
  • 2013 - Sets a then-record for most three-pointers made in a single regular season (272).
  • 2015 - Wins his first NBA Most Valuable Player (MVP) award and leads the Warriors to their first NBA Championship in 40 years.
  • 2016 - Becomes the first unanimous MVP in NBA history after scoring 30.1 points per game and leading the Warriors to a 73-9 regular-season record.
  • 2017 & 2018 - Secures back-to-back NBA Championships with the Golden State Warriors dynasty.
  • 2021 - Breaks Ray Allen's all-time record to become the NBA's career leader in 3-pointers made.
  • 2022 - Wins his 4th NBA Championship and earns his first NBA Finals MVP award.
  • 2024 - Wins a Gold Medal representing Team USA at the Paris Olympic Games, sinking crucial 3-pointers in the final minutes of the gold medal match.
